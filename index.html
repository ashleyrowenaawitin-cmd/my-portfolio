Summary

Making two designs side by side showed me that the layout can stay the same while the mood changes. Colors, fonts and buttons give a website most of its personality.

IronCore showed me how bold colors and heavy text create energy. daisyUI showed me how lighter colors and more text sizes make a site clean and easy to use. Next, I want to turn these Figma designs into working code.
